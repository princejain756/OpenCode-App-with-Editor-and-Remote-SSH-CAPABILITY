## ADR-XXX — Title
**Date:**  
**Phase:**

### Context
What problem required a decision?

### Decision
What was chosen?

### Alternatives considered
What alternatives were evaluated?

### Why
Why was this approach preferred?

### Consequences
What tradeoffs or follow-up work result?
